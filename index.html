01 / About

I build at the intersection of AI, research, and real-world problems.

My path started at Nusrat Senior Secondary School, where curiosity about technology turned into leading the ICT Club and teaching students to code. I became ICT Club President and Lead Instructor, building a 150+ member student technology community and co-founding Coders Hub.

That foundation led me into AI and scientific research. At MRC Unit The Gambia at LSHTM, I worked on computer vision, medical imaging, and machine learning projects including pneumonia detection and CNN classifiers.

Today I build products and contribute to the technology ecosystem. I co-founded Voltify and JobMatch AI, co-organize Hack4Gambia, and lead the Data Science & AI Club and Research Club at the university of The Gambia school of ICT. I share what I learn through talks and my youtube channel CBJtech.
